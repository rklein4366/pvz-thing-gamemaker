ZOMBIES (Zombie.cpp, Zombie.h)
  Basic health=270. Conehead: body=270, cone armor=370. Buckethead: body=270, bucket armor=1100. Cone/bucket visual damage stages follow Zombie::GetHelmDamageIndex: damage stage 1 when current armor health < floor(max armor * 2/3), stage 2 when current armor health < floor(max armor / 3), and armor drops at 0. For the original maxima the strict cutoffs are <246 and <123 cone health, and <733 and <366 bucket health. Pole Vaulter body=500.
  Original PickRandomSpeed parameter ranges: ordinary zombies 0.23..0.37, Pole Vaulter while carrying the pole 0.66..0.68. Current sandbox intentionally reduces ordinary and post-vault movement to 0.16..0.22, while the pre-vault run now uses the original 0.66..0.68. Root translation comes from Reanimation::GetTrackVelocity on `_ground` (frame delta * 0.01 * animation rate), keeping walking motion and foot animation coupled.
  Pole vaulting follows Zombie::UpdateZombiePolevaulter: play `anim_jump` once at 24 fps; compute `mVelX = (mX - plant.mX - 80) / (frame_count / anim_rate * 100)` and translate by that velocity during the jump; then apply `mPosX -= 150` once when the animation ends. The computed landing is locked at takeoff; do not add a separate 150px interpolation over the animation as well.
  Eating: damage 4 every 4 source ticks (average 1 health/source tick).
  Zombie hit flash=25 ticks; body-part detach thresholds arm below 2/3 max health, head below 1/3 max health. Once headless, zombies enter an explicit no-bite branch: they cannot select/eat plants or begin another vault, keep walking, and run the source-style random residual-damage check (Rand(5)==0; 1 body damage, or 3 when body max health >=500) until death. The full death animation is allowed to finish before the object is removed.

TARGETED FIXES (2026-10-23)
  Bite consistency: while a zombie is eating, the reach test compares zombie X against target-plant X with the same -60/+20 bounds as target acquisition. The old test compared plant X against zombie X with the opposite bounds and cancelled eating immediately for plants within the extra range, preventing bite timers from reaching 4 ticks when plants were close.
  Helmet stages: source math from Zombie::GetHelmDamageIndex: return 2 if helmet health < max/3; else return 1 if helmet health < max*2/3; else return 0. The original source updates anim_cone/anim_bucket image overrides to stage 2 and stage 3 when these indices change, then drops the helmet when health reaches 0.
